## 📚 13. Plain-English Glossary of Tech Words

* **APK (Android Package Kit)**: The file format used by Android phones to install mobile apps (just like `.exe` on Windows).
* **CI/CD Pipeline**: An automated software factory that tests and packages code into finished apps automatically.
* **Zero-Knowledge (ZK) Proof**: A mathematical way of proving you have enough money or the correct password without ever revealing your private details to anyone.
* **WebAuthn**: The modern technology that lets you use your phone's fingerprint sensor or laptop face scan instead of typing passwords.
* **Tor Network**: An onion-routing system that bounces your connection through three encrypted computers worldwide so your location stays completely secret.
* **AES-256**: The most secure military-grade encryption standard in the world. It would take a supercomputer billions of years to crack it.
* **Node**: Any phone or computer running this app that helps keep the network fast, decentralized, and secure.
